# Secondary Teachers' Salaries and Work Hours

> [!QUESTION] Đề Bài (Exam Prompt)
> *The table gives information about the salaries and teaching hours of secondary school teachers in five countries (Australia, Denmark, Luxembourg, Japan, Scotland) in 2009. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

---

## 1. 💡 Phân Tích Đề & Dàn Ý Logic (Idea & Outline)
- **Dạng bảng:** Table đối chiếu 4 cột:
  1. *Starting salary* (Lương khởi điểm - USD).
  2. *Salary after 15 years* (Lương sau 15 năm - USD).
  3. *Maximum salary* (Lương kịch trần - USD).
  4. *Annual teaching hours* (Số giờ dạy hàng năm - Hours).
- **Overview:**
  - Giáo viên ở Luxembourg nhận mức thù lao vượt trội hoàn toàn so với 4 quốc gia còn lại ở mọi nấc thang sự nghiệp, trong khi giáo viên Nhật Bản và Úc có giờ giảng dạy cao nhất.
  - Đan Mạch yêu cầu số giờ giảng dạy ít nhất dù trả lương tương đối cao.
- **Thân bài:**
  - **Body 1 (Salaries across career stages):** Luxembourg khởi điểm \$80k, chạm đỉnh \$139k; các nước còn lại khởi điểm từ \$28k - \$34k và kịch trần ở mức \$48k - \$65k.
  - **Body 2 (Teaching hours & So sánh tỷ lệ tương quan):** Nhật Bản (707 giờ), Úc (705 giờ), Scotland (650 giờ), Luxembourg (565 giờ), Đan Mạch (chỉ 480 giờ).

---

## 2. 📝 Bài Viết Mẫu Chuẩn (Band 9.0 Model Answer)

The table compares the remuneration scales at three career benchmarks and the annual teaching workload for secondary school instructors across five industrialized nations in 2009.

Overall, Luxembourg offered by far the most lucrative compensation package for educators at every stage of their careers, while teachers in Japan and Australia endured the heaviest instructional timetables. Conversely, Danish secondary teachers enjoyed the fewest annual teaching hours despite receiving competitive salaries.

In terms of financial compensation, Luxembourg stood in a league of its own. Entry-level teachers in Luxembourg earned an exceptional starting salary of \$80,000, which escalated to \$112,000 after 15 years and culminated at a maximum of \$139,000. In sharp contrast, starting salaries in the other four nations were clustered within a much lower band, ranging from approximately \$28,000 in Japan to \$34,000 in Australia. Maximum earning potential in these four countries plateaued between \$48,000 in Denmark and \$65,000 in Japan.

Regarding operational workload, teaching obligations varied considerably. Japanese and Australian educators logged the highest instructional volume, registering 707 and 705 hours per year, respectively. Scotland occupied an intermediate tier at 650 hours, followed by Luxembourg at 565 hours. Finally, Denmark demanded the lightest instructional burden from its teachers, mandating merely 480 hours annually.

### 🔬 Mổ Xẻ Chi Tiết Mệnh Đề & Kỹ Thuật Liên Kết Câu (Sentence Anatomy & Cohesion)

#### 📌 Câu dài (Body 1 - Cấu trúc so sánh phân cụm và thành ngữ học thuật 'in a league of its own'):
> *"In terms of financial compensation, Luxembourg stood in a league of its own, with entry-level teachers earning an exceptional starting salary of \$80,000, which escalated to \$112,000 after 15 years and culminated at a maximum of \$139,000."*

- **1. Phân tích các Mệnh đề:**
  - **Cụm giới từ chỉ tiêu chí:** `[In terms of financial compensation]`.
  - **Mệnh đề chính:** `[Luxembourg stood in a league of its own]` (Luxembourg đứng ở một đẳng cấp hoàn toàn riêng biệt).
  - **Cụm tuyệt đối bổ trợ số liệu:** `[with entry-level teachers earning an exceptional starting salary of \$80,000]`.
  - **Mệnh đề quan hệ xác định kép:** `[which escalated to \$112,000 after 15 years and culminated at a maximum of \$139,000]`.
- **2. 🔗 Phân tích Liên kết:** Dùng idiom cao cấp `in a league of its own` kết hợp chuỗi động từ thăng tiến `escalated to ... culminated at` làm nổi bật sự vượt trội tuyệt đối của Luxembourg.
- 💡 **Công thức ghép câu:** `In terms of [Metric], [Entity] stood in a league of its own, with [Participants] earning [X], which escalated to [Y] and culminated at a maximum of [Z].`
- 🔄 **3 Phương án Paraphrase:**
  - *Cách 1:* "Luxembourg proved an absolute outlier in teacher earnings, offering \$80,000 for novices, which rose progressively to cap out at \$139,000."
  - *Cách 2:* "Educators in Luxembourg enjoyed unprecedented remuneration, with entry packages of \$80,000 climbing steadily to peak at an unparalleled \$139,000."
  - *Cách 3:* "Far eclipsing its counterparts, Luxembourg compensated secondary teachers with \$80,000 initially, ascending to a ceiling of \$139,000."
