# Plastic Bottle Recycling Process into Fleece Clothing

> [!QUESTION] Đề Bài (Exam Prompt)

- **Trình tự các bước:**

  3. Phân loại theo màu sắc và loại bỏ nắp/nhãn mác (Sorting & stripping).

  6. Rửa sạch và sấy khô (Washing and drying).

  8. Dệt thành các tấm vải sợi (Weaving into fleece fabric).
  9. Cắt may thành quần áo lông cừu nhân tạo thành phẩm (Manufacturing into finished fleece jackets).

## 2. 📝 Bài Viết Mẫu Chuẩn (Band 9.0 Model Answer)

The diagram delineates the multi-stage industrial procedure involved in converting discarded plastic bottles into commercial fleece apparel.

Overall, the recycling cycle comprises nine consecutive phases, beginning with the collection of post-consumer plastic containers and culminating in the fabrication of finished fleece garments. The entire mechanism incorporates both mechanical processing, such as sorting and pulverization, and thermal treatment to transform rigid plastics into synthetic textiles.

The initial stage commences when used plastic bottles are deposited into dedicated recycling receptacles and subsequently trucked to a central processing depot. Here, the containers undergo meticulous sorting by colour and composition, whilst caps and adhesive labels are stripped away. Once classified, the sorted bottles are crushed and compressed into dense, uniform bales to facilitate handling.

In the subsequent industrial stages, these compacted bales are fed into crushing machinery to be shredded into tiny plastic flakes. Following this, the fragments are thoroughly cleansed of contaminants and dehydrated in industrial drying ovens. The clean flakes are then exposed to extreme heat until liquefied, before being extruded through micro-perforated spinnerets to produce fine polyester yarn. In the final phases, this yarn is woven into rolls of fleece cloth, which are ultimately tailored into commercial jackets, ready for retail distribution.

- **1. Phân tích các Mệnh đề:**
  - **Mệnh đề chính bị động:** `[The clean flakes are then exposed to extreme heat]`.

  - **Cụm giới từ chỉ trình tự (before + being + V3):** `[before being extruded through micro-perforated spinnerets to produce fine polyester yarn]`.
- **2. 🔗 Phân tích Liên kết:** Dùng liên từ `until liquefied, before being extruded` mô tả chính xác sự biến đổi vật lý của nhựa từ dạng rắn sang lỏng rồi qua khuôn ép thành sợi chỉ trong một câu liền mạch.
- 💡 **Công thức ghép câu:** `[Raw Material] is then exposed to [Condition] until [State], before being [Passive Verb] through [Machinery] to produce [Finished Intermediate Product].`
