# Online Retail Spending in New Zealand (2003 vs 2013)

> [!QUESTION] Đề Bài (Exam Prompt)
> *The pie charts compare the percentage of online retail sales across four sectors—travel, film/music, books, and clothes—in New Zealand in 2003 and 2013. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.*

---

## 1. 💡 Phân Tích Đề & Dàn Ý Logic (Idea & Outline)
- **Dạng biểu đồ:** 2 Pie Charts so sánh cơ cấu chi tiêu mua sắm online của 4 ngành hàng tại New Zealand qua 10 năm (2003 vs 2013).
- **4 ngành hàng:** Travel, Film/Music, Books, Clothes.
- **Overview:**
  - Ngành Film/Music và Books chứng kiến sự tăng trưởng tỷ trọng, trong đó Film/Music vươn lên thành ngành dẫn đầu năm 2013.
  - Ngược lại, ngành Travel (từng là ngành thống trị năm 2003) và Clothes đều trải qua sự suy giảm về thị phần trực tuyến.
- **Thân bài:**
  - **Body 1 (Travel & Film/Music):** Travel giảm từ 36% xuống 29%; Film/Music tăng từ 21% lên 33% (vượt mặt Travel).
  - **Body 2 (Books & Clothes):** Books tăng từ 19% lên 22%; Clothes giảm từ 24% xuống còn 16% (xuống vị trí thấp nhất).

---

## 2. 📝 Bài Viết Mẫu Chuẩn (Band 9.0 Model Answer)

The two pie charts compare the distribution of online retail expenditure across four commercial categories in New Zealand between 2003 and 2013.

Overall, film and music along with books experienced expansions in their market shares over the decade, with film and music supplanting travel as the leading sector. Conversely, travel and clothing suffered relative contractions, with apparel capturing the smallest portion of online transactions by 2013.

In 2003, travel commanded the largest share of the online shopping market, accounting for over a third of all sales at 36%. However, by 2013, its proportion had shrunk noticeably to 29%. In stark contrast, online sales for film and music surged dramatically from 21% to 33%, allowing this sector to overtake travel and establish itself as the dominant contributor to internet commerce in New Zealand.

Regarding the remaining categories, books and clothes displayed diverging trajectories. In 2003, clothing was the second most popular online purchase at 24%, while books represented 19%. Over the ensuing ten years, the market share for books expanded modestly to 22%, whereas apparel experienced a substantial decline of eight percentage points to finish at just 16%, thus relegated to the least favored online shopping category.

### 🔬 Mổ Xẻ Chi Tiết Mệnh Đề & Kỹ Thuật Liên Kết Câu (Sentence Anatomy & Cohesion)

#### 📌 Câu dài (Body 2 - Cấu trúc phân tích hai chiều đối lập với phân từ thụ động chỉ vị thế):
> *"Over the ensuing ten years, the market share for books expanded modestly to 22%, whereas apparel experienced a substantial decline of eight percentage points to finish at just 16%, thus relegated to the least favored online shopping category."*

- **1. Phân tích các Mệnh đề:**
  - **Cụm trạng từ thời gian:** `[Over the ensuing ten years]`.
  - **Mệnh đề chính 1:** `[the market share for books expanded modestly to 22%]`.
  - **Mệnh đề đối lập song hành (with 'whereas'):** `[whereas apparel experienced a substantial decline of eight percentage points to finish at just 16%]`.
  - **Mệnh đề phân từ quá khứ chỉ hệ quả (Reduced Passive Participle with 'thus'):** `[thus relegated to the least favored online shopping category]`.
- **2. 🔗 Phân tích Liên kết:** Dùng `thus relegated to` để đúc kết vị thế tụt hậu của ngành quần áo sau khi đưa ra số liệu giảm 8 điểm phần trăm.
- 💡 **Công thức ghép câu:** `[Category A] expanded modestly to [X%], whereas [Category B] contracted by [Y points] to finish at [Z%], thus relegated to [Lowest Rank].`
- 🔄 **3 Phương án Paraphrase:**
  - *Cách 1:* "While book purchases appreciated marginally to 22%, clothing dropped by 8% to 16%, slipping to the lowest tier of the online marketplace."
  - *Cách 2:* "During the same decade, books edged up to 22%, in sharp contrast to apparel, which contracted by 8% to end up as the smallest segment at 16%."
  - *Cách 3:* "Books gained modest ground to settle at 22%, whereas online fashion sales slumped to a mere 16%, falling to the bottom of consumer preferences."
