# Notoriety
*n.*
> /ˌnəʊ.tərˈaɪ.ə.ti/

---
**Level:** C1 | **Ngữ cảnh:** Academic | **Chủ đề:** CELEBRITIES' IMPACT ON THE SOCIETY

## 📌 Ý nghĩa (Meaning)
- **[EN]:** The state of being famous or well known for some bad quality.
- **[VN]:** Sự tai tiếng.
