# PHẦN I: KIẾN THỨC TỔNG HỢP WRITING TỪ CƠ BẢN ĐẾN NÂNG CAO

## 1. IELTS Writing Task 1 (Academic Reports): 3 Tầng Nâng Band

### 🟢 Tầng 1: Nền Tảng Chống Mất Điểm Oan (Band 4.5–5.5 $\rightarrow$ 6.0)
* **Quy trình 3 phút trước khi viết:**
  1. **Khóa Thì (Tense Check):** Có năm trong quá khứ (`1990–2010`) $\rightarrow$ **Quá khứ đơn**. Không có năm $\rightarrow$ **Hiện tại đơn**. Có năm tương lai (`2040`) $\rightarrow$ **is projected / forecast to V1**.
  2. **Không đưa ý kiến cá nhân:** Cấm tuyệt đối *"I think", "In my opinion", "luckily", "surprisingly"*.
  3. **Giới từ số liệu bất di bất dịch:**
     - Tăng/giảm **đến** mức nào: `rose TO 50%` | `a rise TO 50%`.
     - Tăng/giảm **một khoảng bao nhiêu**: `rose BY 15%` | `a rise OF 15%`.
     - Đứng **tại** mức nào: `stood AT 40%` | `accounted FOR 30%`.
     - Trong khoảng thời gian: `between 2000 and 2020` / `from 2000 to 2020` / `over a 20-year period`.

### 🟡 Tầng 2: Khung 4 Đoạn Chuẩn Mực Band 6.0–6.5 (`[[Band_Progression_Guide_5_to_7.5]]`)
1. **Introduction (1–2 câu):** Paraphrase *Cái gì + Ở đâu + Khi nào + Đơn vị đo* (`The line graph compares the amount of...` / `The pie charts illustrate the proportion of...`).
2. **Overview (2 câu — LINH HỒN ĐỂ THOÁT BAND 5.0):**
   - Nêu **2 đặc điểm vĩ mô lớn nhất**: (1) Xu hướng chính tăng/giảm & (2) Giá trị cao nhất/thấp nhất hoặc ngoại lệ nổi bật.
   - **CẤM TUYỆT ĐỐI** đưa số liệu cụ thể (`45%`, `1,200 tonnes`) vào đoạn Overview!
3. **Body 1 & Body 2 (Mỗi đoạn 3–4 câu):**
   - **Nguyên tắc Gom Nhóm (Grouping):** Không tả rời từng đường/từng cột từ đầu đến cuối; hãy gom theo **nhóm tăng vs. nhóm giảm**, **nhóm cao nhất vs. thấp nhất**, hoặc **năm đầu vs. năm cuối**.
   - **Map:** Chia theo 2 mốc thời gian hoặc 2 khu vực (Bắc/Đông vs. Nam/Tây) với thì bị động (`was demolished`, `was replaced by`, `has been converted into`).
   - **Process:** Chia làm 2 nửa quy trình (thu hoạch/chuẩn bị $\rightarrow$ chế biến/đóng gói) với **Hiện tại đơn bị động** (`is washed`, `are crushed`) cho quy trình nhân tạo, hoặc **chủ động** (`evaporates`, `lays eggs`) cho vòng đời tự nhiên.

### 🔵 Tầng 3: Bứt Phá Văn Phong Học Thuật Band 7.0–8.5+ (`[[6.1. Task 1 - Data Trends, Comparisons & Processes|6.1]]`, `[[Master_Structures_Task1_and_Task2]]`)
* **So sánh đa tầng & Giao cắt:** Dùng *overtook / surpassed*, *almost threefold*, *dwarfed by comparison at a mere X%*, *respectively*.
* **Rút gọn phân từ & Cụm `with + Noun + V-ing`:** *"Spending on food rose steadily, **with meat accounting for** nearly half of the total."*
* **Tiền đặt câu & Đảo ngữ không gian (Map):** *"To the east of the river stood an old mill, which was later converted into..."*

## 2. IELTS Writing Task 2 (Academic Essays): 3 Tầng Nâng Band

### 🟢 Tầng 1: Sạch Lỗi Câu & Đúng Trọng Tâm Đề Bài (Band 5.0 $\rightarrow$ 6.0)
* **5 Nguyên tắc sinh tử từ `[[Common_Sentence_Errors_and_Punctuation_Guide]]`:**
  1. Trả lời **đủ 100% các vế câu hỏi** trong đề bài (lạc đề 1 vế = tụt *Task Response* xuống 5.0).
  2. Không tách câu bằng dấu phẩy (**Comma Splice**), không dùng `...`, không dùng *Although... but...* hoặc *Because... so...*.
  3. **Checklist 4 phút cuối giờ (Bắt buộc dừng viết ở phút 36 để dò):**
     - (1) Hòa hợp Chủ ngữ – Động từ (`s/es`), (2) Thì & Câu bị động (`be + V3/ed`), (3) Mạo từ & Danh từ đếm được số ít/nhiều, (4) Chính tả & Dấu câu sau *However, Therefore,*.

### 🟡 Tầng 2: Kiến Trúc 4 Đoạn & Đoạn Body PEEL Chuẩn Band 6.0–6.5 (`[[6.2. Task 2 - Introductions & Thesis Statements|6.2]]`, `[[6.3. Task 2 - Body Paragraph Architecture (PEEL)|6.3]]`)
* **Mở bài 2 câu (40–50 từ):**
  - Câu 1 (*Background*): Paraphrase chủ đề đề bài bằng từ đồng nghĩa an toàn.
  - Câu 2 (*Thesis Statement*): Nêu lập trường rõ ràng ngay từ đầu (không mập mờ *"it depends"* ở cuối bài).
* **Mỗi đoạn Thân bài theo đúng khung 4 câu P-E-E-L (90–110 từ/đoạn):**
  1. **P — Point (Topic Sentence):** Nêu 1 luận điểm duy nhất rõ ràng cho cả đoạn.
  2. **E — Explanation:** Giải thích cơ chế *"Tại sao?"* hoặc *"Nếu không có nó thì điều gì xảy ra?"*.
  3. **E — Example:** Đưa 1 ví dụ thực tế quen thuộc (tránh bịa số liệu thống kê giả tạo kiểu *"92.3% in a Harvard study"*).
  4. **L — Link:** Chốt lại tác động ngược về câu hỏi đề bài (*Consequently, / Therefore, / This helps...*).
* **Kết bài 1–2 câu:** Tóm tắt 2 ý chính ở Body + Khẳng định lại Thesis (không đưa ý tưởng hoàn toàn mới vào kết bài).

### 🔵 Tầng 3: Bứt Phá Tư Duy & Học Thuật Band 7.0–8.5+ (`[[6.4. Task 2 - Counter-Argument & Rebuttal Master|6.4]]`, `[[6.6. Writing Progression & Lexical Swaps (Mẫu câu Bậc thang từ Cơ bản đến Nâng cao & Hoán đổi Từ vựng)|6.6]]`)
* **Kỹ thuật Phản biện 3 bước (Concession $\rightarrow$ Pivot $\rightarrow$ Rebuttal):** Thừa nhận mặt hợp lý của phe đối lập (*Admittedly, / Granted,...*) $\rightarrow$ Bẻ lái (*However, this view overlooks...*) $\rightarrow$ Phản bác sắc bén.
* **Ngôn ngữ thận trọng (Academic Hedging):** Dùng *tend to, is likely to, can contribute to, arguably* thay cho khẳng định tuyệt đối (*always, 100%, definitely*).
* **Danh từ hóa (Nominalization) & Shell Nouns:** Biến động từ/tính từ thành cụm danh từ (*"people become obese"* $\rightarrow$ *"rising obesity prevalence"*) và dùng *This phenomenon / Such interventions* nối câu mượt không phụ thuộc *Firstly, Secondly*.

# PHẦN II: KHO 32 CHUỖI VÍ DỤ TIẾN HÓA 4 CẤP ĐỘ (128 VÍ DỤ TỪ DỄ ĐẾN KHÓ)

## A. 16 Chuỗi Ví Dụ Task 1 (Chuỗi 1–16)

### Chuỗi 1 — Mở bài Biểu đồ Đường (Line Graph Introduction)

* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `The line graph illustrates changes in average weekly expenditure on three popular fast foods per British citizen over a 20-year period.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `The line chart delineates the trajectory of weekly per-capita consumption across three fast-food staples in the United Kingdom from 1970 to 1990.`

### Chuỗi 2 — Mở bài Biểu đồ Tĩnh / Bảng (Pie/Bar/Table Introduction)

* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `The two pie charts compare the proportion of energy generated from five different sources in France in 1995 and 2005.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `The pie charts provide a comparative breakdown of French electricity generation across five primary fuel sources in 1995 and 2005.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `The pair of pie charts illustrates the proportional allocation of national energy production among five distinct sources in France across two benchmark years.`

### Chuỗi 3 — Câu Overview cho Biểu đồ có Xu hướng Thời gian
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Overall, hamburgers and pizza went up, while fish and chips went down.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Overall, the consumption of hamburgers and pizza increased significantly, whereas fish and chips saw a downward trend over the period.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `Overall, while hamburger and pizza consumption experienced steady upward trends, fish and chips declined markedly, losing its initial leading position.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Overall, what stands out from the data is a stark divergence: hamburger and pizza intake surged substantially, whereas fish and chips underwent a sharp contraction, relinquishing its early dominance.`

### Chuỗi 4 — Câu Overview cho Biểu đồ So sánh Tĩnh & Ngoại lệ (Outlier)
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Overall, food was the biggest expense in all countries, and leisure was the smallest.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Overall, food and beverages accounted for the largest share of spending in every nation, while leisure consistently made up the lowest figure.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `Overall, all five countries allocated the greatest proportion of consumer spending to food and tobacco, with Turkey and Ireland recording noticeably higher percentages than the rest.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Overall, essential consumables dominated household expenditure across all five nations, whereas leisure and education consistently represented a marginal share, with Sweden posting the most modest spending levels overall.`

### Chuỗi 5 — Miêu tả Xu hướng Tăng Mạnh
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Internet users increased very fast from 10% in 2000 to 80% in 2015.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `The percentage of internet users rose sharply from 10% in 2000 to 80% in 2015.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `Internet adoption experienced dramatic growth over the fifteen-year span, jumping eightfold from a mere 10% in 2000 to 80% by 2015.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Internet penetration witnessed an exponential surge between 2000 and 2015, escalating eightfold from just one-tenth of the population to reach an overwhelming 80%.`

### Chuỗi 6 — Miêu tả Xu hướng Giảm Sâu
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Coal production fell from 60 million tonnes to 15 million tonnes.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Coal production dropped steadily by 45 million tonnes, falling from 60 million in 1990 to 15 million in 2010.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `There was a sharp contraction in coal output, which declined by three-quarters from an initial peak of 60 million tonnes to just 15 million.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Coal extraction underwent a precipitous decline over the two decades, plummeting by 75% to bottom out at a modest 15 million tonnes in 2010.`

### Chuỗi 7 — Miêu tả Điểm Giao Cắt & Vượt Mặt (Overtaking)
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `In 1985, hamburgers became higher than fish and chips.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `In 1985, hamburger consumption overtook fish and chips to become the most popular food choice.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `Having climbed steadily throughout the 1970s, hamburger intake surpassed that of fish and chips in 1985, reaching approximately 300 grams per week.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `A notable crossover occurred around 1985, when surging demand for hamburgers outstripped fish and chips, establishing hamburgers as the preeminent fast food for the remainder of the timeline.`

### Chuỗi 8 — Miêu tả Biến động (Fluctuation) & Đi ngang (Plateau)
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Gas use changed up and down slightly, and nuclear stayed the same.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Gas consumption fluctuated slightly between 25% and 30%, while nuclear energy remained stable at around 10%.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `While natural gas figures oscillated moderately within a narrow range of 25% to 30%, nuclear output plateaued at roughly 10% throughout the decade.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Natural gas exhibited mild volatility, fluctuating within a tight 5-percentage-point band, whereas nuclear power remained virtually stagnant at one-tenth of total supply.`

### Chuỗi 9 — Báo cáo Tỷ lệ Phần trăm (Pie Chart Proportions)
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Water used in farming was 70%, and industry was 20%.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Agriculture accounted for 70% of total water usage, compared to 20% for industrial activities.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `Agriculture consumed the lion's share of water resources at 70%, whereas industrial usage represented a distant second at just one-fifth of the total.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Agricultural irrigation absorbed over two-thirds of total freshwater supply (70%), dwarfing both industrial extraction (20%) and domestic household consumption (10%).`

### Chuỗi 10 — So sánh Gấp bội & Khoảng cách (Table / Bar Chart)
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Teachers in Australia earned $45,000, which is much more than $18,000 in Japan.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Teachers in Australia earned an average salary of \$45,000, which was more than double the figure in Japan (\$18,000).`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `At \$45,000 per year, Australian starting teacher salaries stood at exactly 2.5 times those recorded in Japan (\$18,000).`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Australian educators enjoyed the most lucrative starting remuneration at \$45,000 annually, outstripping their Japanese counterparts by a factor of 2.5.`

### Chuỗi 11 — Liệt kê Số liệu Chuẩn Bằng `respectively`
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `UK had 500 students, France had 400 students, and Spain had 300 students.`

* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Exchange cohorts in the UK, France, and Spain stood at 500, 400, and 300 participants respectively, with the British institution attracting nearly two-thirds more applicants than Spain.`

### Chuỗi 12 — Map: Phá hủy & Thay thế Công trình (Bị động)

## B. 16 Chuỗi Ví Dụ Task 2 (Chuỗi 17–32)

* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Amidst escalating metropolitan gridlock, how municipal authorities should allocate finite infrastructure budgets—between expanding highways and subsidizing mass transit—remains a contentious policy question.`

### Chuỗi 18 — Thesis Statement: Agree / Disagree (Nêu lập trường rõ ràng)

* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `I strongly agree that basic cooking and nutrition lessons should be included in school curricula.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `While academic subjects remain essential, I firmly believe that integrating practical cooking and nutrition education into schools brings long-term health benefits.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Although theoretical coursework forms the bedrock of formal schooling, I wholeheartedly contend that embedding practical nutritional literacy into curricula is indispensable for curbing lifestyle diseases.`

### Chuỗi 19 — Thesis Statement: Discuss Both Views
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Both views have good points, and this essay will discuss them.`

* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `While universal free higher education improves social equality, I contend that charging affordable, income-based tuition helps maintain teaching quality and prevents wasteful public spending.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Although state-funded university education undeniably fosters social mobility, a tiered tuition model coupled with targeted scholarships arguably strikes a more sustainable balance between equity and fiscal prudence.`

### Chuỗi 20 — Thesis Statement: Problem & Solution
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Plastic waste causes problems, and we can solve it in two ways.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `This essay will analyze the main drivers of plastic pollution and propose practical solutions that both governments and consumers can take.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `Single-use plastic pollution stems primarily from cheap packaging and weak recycling systems; however, strict corporate regulations alongside public incentives can steadily reverse this trend.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Driven largely by convenience culture and inadequate municipal recycling, plastic proliferation threatens marine ecosystems, yet coordinated packaging taxes and biodegradable subsidies offer viable remediation.`

### Chuỗi 21 — Thesis Statement: Advantages & Disadvantages
* 🟢 **Cấp 1 (Band 5.0):** `Remote working has some bad points, but the good points are bigger.`
* 🟡 **Cấp 2 (Band 6.0):** `Although remote working can cause feelings of isolation, I believe its advantages in time savings and flexibility clearly outweigh the drawbacks.`
* 🔵 **Cấp 3 (Band 7.0):** `While telecommuting blurs work-life boundaries and reduces spontaneous teamwork, its substantial gains in employee autonomy and reduced commuting clearly tip the balance in its favor.`
* 🟣 **Cấp 4 (Band 8.0+):** `Notwithstanding legitimate concerns regarding professional isolation and work-creep, the transformative benefits of remote employment—ranging from reclaimed personal hours to lowered carbon emissions—decisively outweigh its detriments.`

### Chuỗi 32 — Đoạn Thân Bài PEEL Hoàn Chỉnh 4 Câu (Tiến hóa Cả Đoạn Từ Band 5.0 $\rightarrow$ 8.0+)

* **🟢 Phiên bản Band 5.0 (Ý đúng nhưng câu cụt, từ vựng lặp, ngữ pháp đơn giản):**
  > *"First, traffic is very bad because many people drive cars. Every morning, workers sit on the road for one hour and feel tired. For example, in big cities like Ho Chi Minh City, streets are always full of motorbikes. So people waste a lot of time and air becomes dirty."*

* **🟡 Phiên bản Band 6.0–6.5 (Sạch lỗi, đúng 4 bước PEEL, câu ghép/phức an toàn):**
  > *"The primary cause of urban traffic congestion is the widespread reliance on private vehicles. Because public buses are often slow and unreliable, many commuters choose to drive their own cars or motorbikes to work every day. For instance, in rapidly growing cities like Ho Chi Minh City, millions of motorbikes crowd the streets during morning rush hours. Consequently, commuters waste hours in traffic jams, while vehicle exhaust steadily worsens urban air quality."*

* **🔵 Phiên bản Band 7.0 (Collocations chuẩn xác, liên kết mượt, câu phức đa dạng):**
  > *"Foremost among the drivers of metropolitan gridlock is excessive dependence on private transportation. This stems largely from the fact that inadequate bus and rail networks leave daily commuters with few reliable alternatives for reaching their workplaces on time. A clear case in point is Ho Chi Minh City, where millions of private motorbikes saturate narrow road junctions during peak hours. As a direct result, residents lose valuable productive hours to commuting delays, while unchecked exhaust emissions degrade public air quality."*

* **🟣 Phiên bản Band 8.0+ (Danh từ hóa, Shell Nouns, Mệnh đề phân từ hệ quả):**
  > *"Foremost among the catalysts of metropolitan gridlock is the systemic overreliance on private vehicles over mass transit. This dynamic is predominantly attributable to underfunded public bus and rail networks, which fail to offer commuters punctual and comfortable door-to-door alternatives. A compelling case in point is Ho Chi Minh City, where millions of scooters inundate aging intersections during peak travel windows. Consequently, this infrastructural mismatch inflicts dual damage: squandering immense economic productivity in stalled traffic while exposing urban populations to hazardous particulate emissions."*
