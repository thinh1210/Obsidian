## 🔍 IV. Phân Tích Chuyên Sâu Các Khái Niệm, Idioms & Cấu Trúc So Sánh

---

### 1. Phân Tích Chi Tiết 7 Thành Ngữ & Cụm Từ Thời Trang Cốt Lõi

#### A. **Fit like a glove** /fɪt laɪk ə ɡlʌv/ *(idiom)*
* **Định nghĩa:** To fit someone perfectly in size, shape, and cut *(Vừa vặn như in, như được may đo riêng cho người đó)*.
* **Ví dụ:** "Finding a suit in size 5XL is nearly impossible, but this custom-tailored blazer **fits me like a glove**." *(Tìm một bộ vest cỡ 5XL gần như là bất khả thi, nhưng chiếc áo blazer may đo này lại vừa vặn với tôi như in).*

#### B. **Pull something off** /pʊl ɒf/ *(phrasal verb)*
* **Định nghĩa:** To manage to look attractive or stylish while wearing something bold, unconventional, or difficult to style *(Mặc đẹp và toát lên thần thái dù diện món đồ kén người mặc)*.
* **Ví dụ:** "Not many people can **pull off** neon green pants and chunky boots, but she looks like a high-fashion model." *(Không nhiều người có thể diện đẹp quần xanh neon và bốt hầm hố, nhưng cô ấy trông như một người mẫu thời trang cao cấp).*

#### C. **Hand-me-down** /ˈhænd.miː.daʊn/ *(noun)*
* **Định nghĩa:** A piece of clothing that has been used by an older sibling or relative and given to a younger one *(Quần áo cũ được truyền lại từ anh/chị/người thân)*.
* **Ví dụ:** "As the youngest child in the family, most of my winter jackets were **hand-me-downs** from my elder brother." *(Là con út trong nhà, hầu hết áo khoác mùa đông của tôi đều là đồ mặc lại từ anh trai).*

#### D. **Make a fashion statement** /meɪk ə ˈfæʃ.ən ˈsteɪt.mənt/ *(collocation)*
* **Định nghĩa:** To dress in a distinctive, bold way that expresses a strong personal identity, philosophy, or artistic style *(Diện trang phục độc đáo để khẳng định cá tính hoặc tuyên ngôn phong cách)*.
* **Ví dụ:** "Wearing gothic lace dresses and combat boots to family gatherings is her way of **making a fashion statement**." *(Diện váy ren phong cách gothic và bốt quân đội tới các buổi họp mặt gia đình là cách cô ấy khẳng định tuyên ngôn phong cách của mình).*

#### E. **Fast fashion** vs. **Sustainable fashion** *(Chủ đề thảo luận Part 3)*
* **Fast fashion (Thời trang nhanh):** Quần áo giá rẻ, sản xuất hàng loạt theo xu hướng mới nhất nhưng kém bền (*poor stitching, synthetic fabrics*), dễ rách hỏng sau vài lần giặt và gây ô nhiễm môi trường.
* **Sustainable fashion (Thời trang bền vững):** Trang phục may từ vật liệu thân thiện môi trường (*organic cotton, linen*), chú trọng độ bền, tay nghề thủ công (*craftsmanship*) và điều kiện làm việc công bằng cho công nhân.

#### F. **Fashion victim** /ˈfæʃ.ən ˈvɪk.tɪm/ *(noun)*
* **Định nghĩa:** Someone who blindly follows every passing trend regardless of whether it flatters their body shape, suits their personality, or is practical *(Nạn nhân thời trang — người mù quáng chạy theo mọi trào lưu dù không hợp với mình)*.

#### G. **Presentable** /prɪˈzen.tə.bəl/ & **Attire** /əˈtaɪər/ *(formal words)*
* **Presentable:** Gọn gàng, sạch sẽ, tươm tất và phù hợp với hoàn cảnh.
* **Attire:** Trang phục (từ trang trọng hơn của *clothes*), thường đi kèm: *formal attire, business casual attire, festive attire*.

### 2. Các Cấu Trúc So Sánh & Đối Chiếu Ăn Điểm Trong Topic Quần Áo (Grammar Tools)

Khi so sánh giữa Nam - Nữ, Người trẻ - Người già, Đồ đắt - Đồ rẻ, hãy dùng các mẫu câu này:

* **1. So sánh hơn / kém:**
  - `X is far more [adjective] than Y`: *"Women's fashion is **far more diverse** than men's clothing."*
  - `X is less [adjective] than Y`: *"Men's clothing choices are usually **less complicated** than women's."*
* **2. Mẫu câu Tương phản (Contrast với While / Whereas):**
  - `A tend to [do X], whereas B is more inclined to [do Y]`:
  - *"Young people **tend to follow fast fashion trends**, **whereas** the older generation **is more inclined to stick to classic, durable pieces**."*
* **3. Mẫu câu Nhượng bộ (While it is true that... price does not necessarily equate to...):**
  - *"**While it is true that luxury brands use premium fabrics**, price **does not necessarily equate to superior quality**."*

### Câu 1: Do you prefer to shop for clothes online or in physical stores?

> *(Tích hợp câu chuyện cá nhân có thật của bạn: Người có phom dáng ngoại cỡ 4XL-5XL, cỡ giày 48, khó tìm đồ ở TTTM nên mua sắm online là cứu cánh).*

#### 🟡 Cấp độ 1: DỄ (Foundation - Band 4.5 - 5.5)
> *"I definitely prefer to buy my clothes online. The main reason is that I have a very big body size, usually 4XL or 5XL, and my shoe size is 48. When I go to normal stores or shopping malls, they never have clothes that fit me. Online shops have special stores for big sizes, so it is much easier for me to find what I need."*
* **Điểm sáng:** Câu từ mạch lạc, chân thực, nêu rõ lý do cá nhân (*big body size, 4XL or 5XL, shoe size 48, normal stores don't have my size*). Rất dễ nói và phát âm.

#### 🟢 Cấp độ 2: VỪA (Intermediate - Band 6.0 - 6.5)
> *"I have a strong preference for shopping for clothes online rather than visiting physical stores. To be honest, finding suitable clothing in traditional malls is almost impossible for me because I wear oversized clothes — usually around 4XL or 5XL — and my shoe size is 48. Physical stores in Vietnam rarely stock these special sizes, whereas online platforms offer specialized shops catering to plus-size individuals."*
* **Điểm nâng cấp:** Dùng từ nối tự nhiên (*To be honest, whereas*), từ vựng chính xác (*physical stores, traditional malls, stock special sizes, plus-size individuals*).

#### 🔵 Cấp độ 3: KHÓ (Advanced - Band 7.0 - 8.0+)
> *"Without a doubt, e-commerce is my go-to avenue for apparel shopping. Given that I have a particularly imposing stature — requiring sizes 4XL to 5XL and shoe size 48 — brick-and-mortar boutiques and shopping malls almost never cater to my proportions. Browsing specialized online retailers allows me to effortlessly source customized, **oversized baggy streetwear** that **fits me like a glove**, sparing me the frustration of empty-handed mall trips."*
* **Phân tích bước chuyển đổi (Step-by-step Upgrade):**
  - *buy online* ➡️ *shopping online rather than physical stores* ➡️ **e-commerce is my go-to avenue for apparel shopping**.
  - *I have a big body* ➡️ *I wear oversized clothes* ➡️ **given that I have an imposing stature**.
  - *normal stores* ➡️ *traditional physical stores* ➡️ **brick-and-mortar boutiques**.
  - *clothes fit me well* ➡️ *find clothes in my size* ➡️ **source customized streetwear that fits me like a glove**.

### Câu 2: How often do you buy new clothes?

#### 🟡 Cấp độ 1: DỄ (Foundation - Band 4.5 - 5.5)
> *"I don't buy clothes very often, only about three or four times a year. However, every time I go shopping, I buy a lot of things at once. Usually, I buy new T-shirts and shoes before big festivals like Tet holiday so that I can look fresh and neat."*
* **Điểm sáng:** Trả lời trực diện vào tần suất (*3-4 times a year, buy a lot at once, before Tet holiday*).

#### 🟢 Cấp độ 2: VỪA (Intermediate - Band 6.0 - 6.5)
> *"I only purchase new clothing roughly three or four times each year, but I tend to buy in bulk. Instead of shopping on a monthly basis, I save up and do a major wardrobe haul before special occasions, particularly before Tet holiday. I usually stock up on my favourite basics like plain oversized T-shirts and durable sneakers."*
* **Điểm nâng cấp:** Từ vựng tự nhiên (*buy in bulk, wardrobe haul, on a monthly basis, stock up on basics*).

#### 🔵 Cấp độ 3: KHÓ (Advanced - Band 7.0 - 8.0+)
> *"I would describe myself as an infrequent yet decisive shopper. I only revamp my **wardrobe** about three or four times annually, predominantly in the run-up to major festive occasions like the Lunar New Year. When I do shop, I indulge in a major haul, stocking up on essential **wardrobe staples** such as heavyweight cotton tees and footwear, rather than succumbing to the impulse of weekly **fast fashion** trends."*
* **Phân tích bước chuyển đổi (Step-by-step Upgrade):**
  - *I buy 3-4 times a year* ➡️ *I only purchase new clothing roughly 3-4 times* ➡️ **an infrequent yet decisive shopper who revamps the wardrobe annually**.
  - *buy a lot at once* ➡️ *do a major wardrobe haul* ➡️ **indulge in a major haul, stocking up on essential wardrobe staples**.

### Câu 3: Do you think the clothes we wear can reflect our personality?

#### 🟡 Cấp độ 1: DỄ (Foundation - Band 4.5 - 5.5)
> *"Yes, I totally think so. What we choose to wear says a lot about who we are. For example, people who like simple and quiet styles often wear plain and dark colors, while people with energetic personalities often choose bright colors and unusual patterns."*
* **Điểm sáng:** Dùng ví dụ đối chiếu đơn giản mà hiệu quả (*plain and dark colors vs bright colors and unusual patterns*).

#### 🟢 Cấp độ 2: VỪA (Intermediate - Band 6.0 - 6.5)
> *"Yes, absolutely. Clothes serve as a visual reflection of one's personality and mood. The colors, patterns, and cuts we choose communicate how we perceive ourselves. For instance, someone who wears tailored, minimalist outfits usually projects a serious and organized personality, while someone who loves loose, baggy streetwear probably values comfort and freedom."*
* **Điểm nâng cấp:** Cấu trúc tốt (*visual reflection of personality, communicate how we perceive ourselves, projects an organized personality*).

#### 🔵 Cấp độ 3: KHÓ (Advanced - Band 7.0 - 8.0+)
> *"**Undoubtedly, yes.** Attire is essentially a silent yet potent medium of **self-expression**. The deliberate choice of textures, silhouettes, and color palettes projects an individual's inner mindset and aesthetic sensibility to the world. A preference for minimalist, well-fitted garments often mirrors a disciplined and understated **demeanor**, whereas an affinity for bold prints and vintage pieces signifies a creative soul who isn't afraid to **make a fashion statement**."*
* **Phân tích bước chuyển đổi (Step-by-step Upgrade):**
  - *clothes show who we are* ➡️ *visual reflection of one's personality* ➡️ **a silent yet potent medium of self-expression**.
  - *colors and patterns* ➡️ *cuts and styles* ➡️ **deliberate choice of textures, silhouettes, and color palettes**.
  - *behavior/personality* ➡️ *organized personality* ➡️ **understated demeanor / make a fashion statement**.

## 🎤 VI. Part 2: Speaking Cue Card Theo 2 Cấp Độ

You should say:
- Who this person is
- How you know him/her
- What kind of clothes he/she wears

---

### 🟢 Bản VỪA (Mục tiêu Band 5.5 - 6.5): Kể về cô em họ An (Punk & Gothic Style)

> *"Today I'd like to tell you about my cousin An, who is probably the most uniquely dressed person I know.*
> 
> *An and I are the same age, and we grew up together, so I have known her all my life. While most young women in Vietnam wear simple, feminine dresses or traditional Ao Dai during festive occasions, An has a totally different fashion taste: she loves the punk and gothic styles.*
> 
> *Her wardrobe is filled with second-hand items and vintage pieces that she buys online. Whenever there is a family gathering or a wedding, she stands out immediately. For example, during last Tet holiday, while everyone wore bright red and yellow clothes, An showed up wearing an oversized black leather jacket over a long red velvet skirt, paired with heavy combat boots, lace gloves, and dark purple lipstick.*
> 
> *I consider her clothing choices unusual because they completely break traditional expectations, especially in Vietnamese family culture where modesty and cheerful colors are preferred. Some older relatives in my family were quite shocked at first. However, I really admire her boldness because she uses fashion as a way of self-expression and is never afraid to show her true personality."*

---

### 🔵 Bản KHÓ (Bứt phá Band 7.5 - 8.0+): Kể về cậu bạn sinh viên thiết kế thời trang Duy (Runway & Avant-garde Style)

> *"Today I'd like to talk about an acquaintance of mine from university named Duy, whose sartorial taste is arguably the most eccentric and avant-garde I have ever encountered.*
> 
> *Duy is an undergraduate majoring in fashion design, which largely explains his fearless approach to clothing. We belong to the same English-speaking society on campus, and whenever he turns up for weekly meetings, his outfits look as though they were plucked straight from an international runway.*
> 
> *Rather than conforming to the standard student uniform of hoodies and jeans, Duy treats his body as a blank canvas. He specializes in deconstructed and gender-neutral aesthetics. For instance, I vividly recall him wearing an oversized jacket crafted entirely from recycled denim patches, paired with asymmetrical trousers where one leg was visibly longer than the other. To complement his ensembles, he regularly sports mismatched sneakers painted by hand and dyes his hair in striking pastel tones like silver or emerald green.*
> 
> *I consider his fashion choices exceptionally unusual because they openly defy conventional **dress codes**, especially within an academic environment. While some conservative peers perceive his style as overly dramatic, I have immense admiration for his creative conviction. He refuses to be a **fashion victim** of commercial trends; instead, he uses his **attire** as an unapologetic vehicle for **self-expression**, redefining what contemporary streetwear can look like."*

### Câu 1: Do you think men and women have the same view of clothing?

> *"**I would argue there are still noticeable discrepancies, although the gap is narrowing.** Historically, women have been socially encouraged to place greater emphasis on aesthetic nuance, paying meticulous attention to color harmony, silhouette, and **accessorizing**. Furthermore, the women's fashion market offers an overwhelming breadth of seasonal trends, prompting them to curate a dynamic **wardrobe**. Conversely, men have traditionally prioritized functionality and ergonomic comfort, often sticking to a utilitarian rotation of basic tees and trousers.*
> 
> *That being said, with the ascendancy of social media platforms like Instagram and TikTok, contemporary men are becoming far more **fashion-conscious**. ### Câu 2: Do you agree that expensive clothes are always better quality than cheap clothes?

> *"**Not necessarily.** While it is undeniable that high-end designer labels frequently utilize superior raw textiles, such as mulberry silk or pure cashmere, alongside impeccable stitching, an exorbitant price tag does not automatically guarantee exceptional durability. In many instances, consumers are merely paying a premium for prestige branding, marketing campaigns, and logo recognition rather than genuine craftsmanship.*
> 
> *On the other hand, while mass-market **fast fashion** brands often produce flimsy garments that deteriorate after a few laundry cycles, there is a flourishing market of boutique, local manufacturers offering **sustainable fashion** at accessible price points. Therefore, quality is determined by fabric composition and ethical production rather than the retail price alone."*

* **Key Collocations:** *impeccable stitching, exorbitant price tag, paying a premium for prestige branding, genuine craftsmanship, mass-market fast fashion, sustainable fashion, fabric composition.*

### Câu 3: In what ways do you think clothing choices differ between younger and older generations?

> *"**The generational divide in sartorial choices is profoundly stark.** Youth culture is largely driven by a thirst for novelty and individuality. Younger demographics gravitate toward bold streetwear, oversized silhouettes, and eclectic thrifted items, viewing fashion as an essential canvas for **self-expression** and non-conformity. They eagerly embrace experimental micro-trends and fluid aesthetics.*
> 
> *In sharp contrast, older generations generally prioritize decorum, dignity, and longevity. They tend to favor neutral palettes, timeless cuts, and conservative fabrics that exude modesty. In a Vietnamese cultural context, older citizens instinctively adhere to established social etiquette, opting for understated shirts or traditional Ao Dai during momentous occasions. For them, clothing is a demonstration of respect toward the collective community, whereas for the youth, it is an outward celebration of the individual self."*

* **Key Collocations:** *generational divide, sartorial choices, thirst for novelty, non-conformity, decorum and dignity, timeless cuts, conservative fabrics, social etiquette.*
