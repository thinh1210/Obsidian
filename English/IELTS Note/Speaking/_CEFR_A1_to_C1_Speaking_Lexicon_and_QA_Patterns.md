## 📊 PHẦN I: BẢNG PHỔ TỪ VỰNG & CỤM TỪ CHỨC NĂNG TRẢI DÀI TỪ A1 ĐẾN C1 (FUNCTIONAL LEXICON SPECTRUM)

### 1. Diễn Đạt Sở Thích, Đam Mê & Sự Không Thích (Preferences, Passions & Dislikes)

| Sắc thái ý nghĩa | A1 (Sơ cấp) | A2 (Tiền trung cấp) | B1 (Trung cấp) | B2 (Trung cao cấp) | C1 (Cao cấp / Tự nhiên) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thích / Yêu thích** | *I like / I love* | *I really enjoy / I'm interested in* | *I'm keen on / I'm fond of* | *I'm passionate about / I have a soft spot for* | **I have an insatiable penchant for / I'm an avid devotee of** |
| **Cực kỳ mê** | *I like ... very much* | *I'm crazy about* | *I'm a big fan of* | *I'm thoroughly captivated by* | **I harbor a profound affinity for / It's right up my alley** |
| **Thích A hơn B** | *I like A more than B* | *I prefer A to B* | *I'd rather do A than B* | *I lean heavily towards A over B* | **I gravitate unequivocally towards A rather than B** |
| **Không thích** | *I don't like* | *I hate / I dislike* | *I'm not a fan of* | *It doesn't really appeal to me* | **It's not really my cup of tea / I have an aversion to** |
| **Không chịu nổi** | *I really hate* | *I can't stand* | *I find it annoying* | *It drives me up the wall* | **It is an absolute pet peeve of mine / I find it utterly intolerable** |
| **Bắt đầu thích** | *start to like* | *begin to enjoy* | *get into (a hobby)* | *develop a taste for / take up* | **cultivate an enduring appreciation for / acquire a taste for** |

## 🎯 PHẦN II: 10 DẠNG CÂU HỎI KINH ĐIỂN & KHUNG TRẢ LỜI MẪU TỪ A1 ĐẾN C1 (QUESTION ARCHETYPES & TEMPLATES)

Dưới đây là 10 dạng câu hỏi bao phủ **95% đề thi IELTS Speaking Part 1 & Part 3**. Mỗi dạng có công thức lắp ghép và câu trả lời minh họa ở 3 cấp độ: **A1-A2 (Nền tảng)**, **B1-B2 (Trung cấp vững)**, và **C1 (Học thuật / Tự nhiên cao cấp)**.

### Dạng 1: Hỏi Về Mức Độ Yêu Thích (Liking / Preference Questions)
* **Dấu hiệu câu hỏi:** *"Do you like [X]?"*, *"Are you interested in [X]?"*, *"What is your favorite [X]?"*

#### 📐 Khung Công Thức Lắp Ghép (Sentence Frames):
* 🟢 **Mức A1 - A2:** `Yes, I really like [X]. I usually [Action with X] when I have free time because it makes me feel [Adjective].`
* 🟡 **Mức B1 - B2:** `Definitely, I'm quite keen on [X]. Whenever I get the chance, I love [V-ing] because it's a fantastic way to [Benefit 1] and [Benefit 2].`
* 🔵 **Mức C1:** `Without a shadow of a doubt, I have a deep affinity for [X]. Not only does it serve as a therapeutic outlet to [Benefit 1], but it also enables me to [Benefit 2].`

#### 💬 Ví Dụ Minh Họa (Câu hỏi: *Do you like reading books?*):
* 🟢 **A1 - A2:** *"Yes, I really like reading books. I usually read stories in my bedroom before I go to sleep because it makes me feel relaxed and helps me learn new words."*
* 🟡 **B1 - B2:** *"Definitely, I'm quite keen on reading, especially detective novels and self-help books. Whenever I have some spare time on weekends, I love curling up with a good book because it's a fantastic way to unwind and broaden my horizons."*
* 🔵 **C1:** *"Without a shadow of a doubt, I consider myself an avid bibliophile. Immersing myself in non-fiction literature not only serves as a deeply cathartic outlet to disconnect from digital screens, but it also sharpens my analytical thinking and enriches my worldview."*

### Dạng 2: Hỏi Về Thói Quen & Tần Suất (Frequency & Habit Questions)
* **Dấu hiệu câu hỏi:** *"How often do you [X]?"*, *"When do you usually [X]?"*, *"Do you often [X]?"*

#### 📐 Khung Công Thức Lắp Ghép:

* 🟡 **Mức B1 - B2:** `Well, it depends on my schedule, but generally I try to [Action] on a regular basis. If I'm not too busy with work, I'll [Action] at least [X times a week] in order to [Purpose].`
* 🔵 **Mức C1:** `I make a conscious effort to incorporate [X] into my daily regimen, typically [Frequency]. However, during hectic periods when I'm swamped with deadlines, I may have to scale back to [Lower frequency].`

#### 💬 Ví Dụ Minh Họa (Câu hỏi: *How often do you play sports or exercise?*):

### Dạng 3: Hỏi Lựa Chọn Giữa A và B (Comparing Two Options: "Do you prefer A or B?")

#### 💬 Ví Dụ Minh Họa (Câu hỏi: *Do you prefer eating at home or eating out at restaurants?*):
