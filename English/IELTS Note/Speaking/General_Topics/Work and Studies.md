## 🔍 IV. Phân Tích Chuyên Sâu Các Từ Vựng Nâng Cao

### 1. **Demanding** /dɪˈmɑːn.dɪŋ/ *(Adjective - C1)*
* **Định nghĩa:** Needing a lot of time, attention, or physical/mental effort *(Đòi hỏi cao, hao tổn nhiều tâm trí hoặc thể lực)*.
* **Tại sao dùng thay *Difficult*?** *Difficult* chỉ mang tính chất bài toán khó giải. *Demanding* miêu tả tính chất công việc hoặc một ông sếp có yêu cầu rất khắt khe, áp lực cao.
* **Collocations:** *a demanding boss, physically demanding, intellectually demanding job*.
* **Lỗi phát âm:** Trọng âm rơi vào âm tiết thứ hai `/ˈmɑːn/`, không nhấn âm thứ nhất.

### 2. **Lucrative** /ˈluː.krə.tɪv/ *(Adjective - C1)*
* **Định nghĩa:** Producing a great deal of profit *(Sinh lời cao, mang lại thu nhập béo bở)*.
* **Sắc thái:** Dùng cho nghề nghiệp, thương vụ làm ăn hoặc dự án đầu tư (*a lucrative market, a lucrative career*).
* **Phân biệt:** *Rich* dùng cho người (`He is rich`); còn *Lucrative* dùng cho công việc, cơ hội hoặc hợp đồng kinh doanh (`a lucrative proposition`).

### 3. **Burnout** /ˈbɜːn.aʊt/ *(Noun - C1)*
* **Định nghĩa:** The state of physical or mental exhaustion caused by prolonged stress or overwork *(Hội chứng kiệt sức nghề nghiệp)*.
* **Cụm thông dụng:** `suffer from burnout`, `experience occupational burnout`, `reach the point of burnout`.

### 4. **Hands-on** /ˌhændzˈɒn/ *(Adjective - B2/C1)*
* **Định nghĩa:** Involving direct, active participation or practical experience rather than theory *(Thực hành trực tiếp, bắt tay vào việc thay vì lý thuyết suông)*.
* **Ví dụ đối chiếu:** Lý thuyết trường học (*theoretical knowledge*) ➡️ Kinh nghiệm thực chiến (*hands-on experience*).

## 🧠 V. Phân Tích Cấu Trúc Câu Theo Sub-Themes (Sentence Patterns)

### 1. Theme Môi trường làm việc & Đồng nghiệp (Workplace Culture)
* **Mẫu câu Đảo ngữ Điều kiện loại 2 (Conditional Inversion Type 2):**
  - *Template:* `Were it not for [Supportive factor], surviving [Demanding situation] would be virtually impossible.`
  - *Ví dụ mẫu:* *"**Were it not for the unwavering support of my colleagues**, managing such a grueling workload would be virtually impossible."*

### 2. Theme Động lực nghề nghiệp (Career Ambitions)
* **Mẫu câu Chẻ với 'What' (Pseudo-cleft Sentence):**
  - *Template:* `What truly propels me to excel in my field is [Core intrinsic motivator].`
  - *Ví dụ mẫu:* *"**What truly propels me to excel in my profession is** not merely the financial compensation, but the sheer satisfaction of solving intricate problems."*

### 3. Theme Áp lực thi cử / Deadline (Academic Pressure)
* **Mẫu câu So sánh kép (Double Comparative):**
  - *Template:* `The closer [Exam/Deadline] approaches, the more imperative it becomes to [Action].`
  - *Ví dụ mẫu:* *"**The closer final examinations approach, the more imperative it becomes to** manage my revision schedule meticulously."*

### Câu 1: Do you work or are you a student?

#### 🟢 Level 1: Dễ (Band 5.0 - 5.5):
> *"Currently, I am working as a graphic designer for an advertising agency in my city. I graduated from university two years ago and I have been doing this job since then. I like it because I can be creative every day."*
* **Nhận xét Level 1:** Câu ngắn, đúng thì quá khứ và hiện tại hoàn thành, từ vựng rõ ý (*graphic designer, graduated, creative*).

#### 🟡 Level 2: Vừa (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"Well, at the moment I am working full-time as a visual designer at a marketing firm. Since finishing my bachelor's degree two years ago, I've been focusing on developing my practical design skills and handling various client projects on a daily basis."*
* **Nhận xét Level 2:** Trôi chảy hơn, dùng cụm *working full-time, bachelor's degree, practical design skills, handling various client projects*.

#### 🔵 Level 3: Khó (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"Presently, I'm employed as a full-time UI/UX designer at an international digital agency. Having completed my tertiary education a couple of years back, I decided to immerse myself immediately in the creative industry, which has offered me an abundance of **hands-on experience** in crafting user-centric digital products."*
* **Phân tích nâng cấp (Upgrade Analysis):**
  - *working as* ➡️ **employed as a full-time... at...**
  - *graduated from university* ➡️ **Having completed my tertiary education** (phân từ rút gọn).
  - *learn by doing* ➡️ **immerse myself immediately in... offering an abundance of hands-on experience**.

### Câu 4: Do you prefer working individually or in a team?

#### 🟢 Level 1: Dễ (Band 5.0 - 5.5):
> *"I think I prefer working in a team because two heads are better than one. When we work together, we can share ideas and finish the work faster."*

#### 🟡 Level 2: Vừa (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"Generally speaking, I lean towards teamwork. Collaborating with others enables us to brainstorm more diverse perspectives and divide heavy tasks effectively. That said, I still appreciate solitary time when I need deep concentration."*

#### 🔵 Level 3: Khó (Band 7.5 - 8.5):

* **Phân tích nâng cấp (Upgrade Analysis):**
  - *lean towards* ➡️ **It largely depends on the nature of the task** (tư duy linh hoạt đa chiều).
  - *work together* ➡️ **cross-functional collaboration fosters synergy and multifaceted problem-solving**.
  - *need quiet* ➡️ **execution phase requiring laser-sharp focus**.

## 🎤 VII. Part 2: Cue Card, Dàn Ý 4 Bước & Full Model Speeches

### Đề bài: Describe a difficult project or task you worked on at work or university
You should say:
- What the task/project was
- Who you worked with
- What made it difficult
- And explain how you felt after completing it

#### 🧭 Dàn ý 4 bước (Mindmap 1 phút):
1. **Introduction / Context:** Khóa luận tốt nghiệp hoặc dự án ra mắt ứng dụng di động cho công ty (`final year capstone project / mobile app rollout`).
2. **Teamwork / Roles:** Nhóm 4 thành viên, bản thân phụ trách giao diện và người dùng (`spearhead the user interface design`).
3. **Major Obstacles:** Dữ liệu bị lỗi, thời hạn gấp rút, bất đồng quan điểm (`severe time constraints, conflicting viewpoints, unexpected technical glitches`).
4. **Outcome & Feelings:** Thức trắng đêm chạy nước rút, hoàn thành xuất sắc và cảm thấy nhẹ nhõm vô bờ (`burn the midnight oil, immense sense of accomplishment, sense of relief`).

---

#### 🟡 Bài Mẫu Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"I would like to talk about my university graduation project that I completed last year. It was a research project about e-commerce trends in Vietnam, and I had to collaborate with three other classmates.*
> 
> *The main challenge was that we had to collect survey responses from over 500 consumers in a very short timeframe of just three weeks. On top of that, each team member had different schedules, so finding a common time to meet and analyze the statistical data was extremely difficult. There were moments when we argued about the best way to structure our final report.*
> 
> *To overcome this, we set up clear deadlines for each person and held daily online catch-ups. We literally worked day and night in the final week to assemble the final presentation.*
> 
> *When we finally received an A grade from our professors, I felt an enormous sense of relief. It taught me how to communicate better under pressure."*

---

#### 🔵 Bài Mẫu Level 3 (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"I'm going to recount a rather demanding project that took place during my final semester at university, which was our comprehensive **capstone project** focused on optimizing user retention for digital banking services.
> 
> I was tasked with leading a cross-disciplinary team of four, where my primary responsibility was overseeing interface optimization and data synthesis. What rendered the endeavor exceptionally grueling was twofold: first, we were subjected to an unforgiving three-week turnaround; second, halfway through the timeline, our primary dataset was corrupted, forcing us to pivot and re-conduct our quantitative analysis from scratch.
> 
> Tensions inevitably flared due to divergent creative visions and mounting academic stress. To mitigate the friction, I instituted daily agile check-ins and reallocated responsibilities in accordance with each individual's technical proficiencies. For roughly ten consecutive days, our team practically **burned the midnight oil** in the campus library, subsisting on endless cups of espresso.

Completing that arduous assignment served as a watershed moment for me; it not only fortified my crisis management acumen but also instilled in me the conviction that resilience is the ultimate prerequisite for overcoming adversity."*

## 🎙️ VIII. Part 3: Câu Hỏi Thảo Luận Mở Rộng (Discussion Questions)

### Q1: What factors should people consider when choosing a career path?

#### 🟡 Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"I think salary is definitely a major factor because everyone needs money to pay for bills and living expenses. However, personal interest and job satisfaction are also crucial because if you hate your job, you won't be able to stay in it for long."*

#### 🔵 Level 3 (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"From my perspective, career selection requires a delicate calibration between **financial remuneration** and intrinsic passion. While a competitive compensation package is vital for securing financial autonomy, pursuing a vocation solely for material gains frequently leads to psychological disillusionment. Additionally, long-term **growth prospects** and corporate alignment with one's personal values should carry substantial weight in that decision-making process."*

---

### Q2: Do you believe that AI and automated systems will replace humans in the future workforce?

#### 🟡 Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"In my opinion, AI will definitely replace repetitive manual jobs like data entry or basic factory work. But for creative jobs or positions that need human feelings, like doctors or teachers, machines can never completely take over."*

#### 🔵 Level 3 (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"I would argue that while artificial intelligence will inevitably automate mundane, algorithmically predictable assignments, it will act more as a catalyst for **workforce augmentation** rather than wholesale human displacement. Roles requiring high emotional intelligence, ethical deliberation, and nuanced creative ingenuity will remain uniquely within the human domain. Consequently, professionals who master AI integration will outperform those who resist technological adaptation."*

---

### Q3: Should corporations be held legally liable if exploitative corporate culture precipitates clinical burnout and psychological breakdown among employees?

#### 🟡 Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
