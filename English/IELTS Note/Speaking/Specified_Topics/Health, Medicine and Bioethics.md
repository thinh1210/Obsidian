## 🔍 IV. Phân Tích Chuyên Sâu Các Từ Vựng Nâng Cao

### 1. **Bioethics** /ˌbaɪ.əʊˈeθ.ɪks/ *(Noun - C2)*
* **Định nghĩa:** The study of the ethical, social, and legal issues that arise in biomedicine, biomedical research, and genetic modification *(Đạo đức sinh học)*.
* **Collocations:** *contentious bioethical debate, breach bioethical codes, bioethical oversight*.
* **Ví dụ:** *"Cloning and synthetic biology pose unprecedented **bioethical** dilemmas for modern legislation."*

### 2. **Prophylaxis** /prɒf.ɪˈlæk.sɪs/ *(Noun - C2)*
* **Định nghĩa:** Action taken to prevent disease, especially by specified means or against a specified malady *(Biện pháp phòng ngừa bệnh tật mang tính hệ thống)*.
* **Sắc thái:** Mang tính chuyên môn y khoa cao hơn từ `prevention`. Dùng khi đề cập đến vaccine, dinh dưỡng học hoặc thói quen ngăn ngừa bệnh tim mạch.

### 3. **Hypokinetic** /ˌhaɪ.pəʊ.kɪˈnet.ɪk/ *(Adjective - C2)*
* **Định nghĩa:** Pertaining to or caused by abnormally decreased muscular movement or activity *(Bệnh lý suy giảm vận động cơ bắp)*.
* **Ví dụ:** *"Cardiovascular diseases and type-2 diabetes are quintessential **hypokinetic afflictions** of the digital age."*

## 🧠 V. Phân Tích Cấu Trúc Câu Theo Sub-Themes (Sentence Patterns)

### 1. Theme Y học dự phòng vs Trị bệnh (Preventive vs Curative Healthcare)
* **Mẫu câu Đảo ngữ Điều kiện (Inversion Conditional Type 2):**
  - *Template:* `Were governments to divert funds from [Curative treatment] to [Prophylactic education], society would avert [Systemic crisis].`
  - *Ví dụ mẫu:* *"**Were public health authorities to divert substantive funding from late-stage curative interventions toward early prophylactic education**, nations **would avert the impending fiscal collapse of their healthcare infrastructure**."*

### 2. Theme Công nghệ can thiệp di truyền (Genetic Intervention)
* **Mẫu câu Chẻ (Pseudo-cleft Sentence):**
  - *Template:* `What renders genome editing exceptionally controversial is [Bioethical boundary].`
  - *Ví dụ mẫu:* *"**What renders embryonic CRISPR genome editing exceptionally contentious is** the nebulous ethical demarcation between eradicating hereditary illnesses and fabricating genetically superior human beings."*

## 🗣️ VI. Part 1: Câu Trả Lời Mẫu Đối Chiếu Đa Tầng (Level 1 ➡️ Level 2 ➡️ Level 3)

---

### Câu 1: What do you do to keep yourself healthy?

#### 🟢 Level 1: Dễ (Band 5.0 - 5.5):
> *"To keep fit, I try to drink at least two liters of water every day and go for a brisk jog in the park near my house. I also try to avoid fast food like fried chicken and burgers."*

#### 🟡 Level 2: Vừa (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"I maintain a disciplined wellness routine. I make a conscious effort to consume a balanced diet rich in leafy greens and lean proteins. Additionally, I hit the gym four times a week to combine cardiovascular exercises with resistance training."*

#### 🔵 Level 3: Khó (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"I adopt a holistic paradigm centered on **proactive prophylaxis**. Beyond adhering to an unadulterated, nutrient-dense diet and incorporating high-intensity interval training, I place immense premium on safeguarding seven hours of deep sleep. Preserving circadian alignment is the ultimate prerequisite for maintaining metabolic equilibrium and cognitive acuity."*
* **Phân tích nâng cấp (Upgrade Analysis):**
  - *keep fit* ➡️ **adopt a holistic paradigm centered on proactive prophylaxis**.
  - *eat vegetables and exercise* ➡️ **nutrient-dense diet, high-intensity interval training, metabolic equilibrium**.

---

### Câu 2: Do you prefer exercising in a gym or outdoors?

#### 🟢 Level 1: Dễ (Band 5.0 - 5.5):
> *"I prefer running outdoors because I love breathing fresh morning air and seeing green trees. Working out in a gym feels a bit boring and crowded to me."*

#### 🟡 Level 2: Vừa (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"I definitely favor outdoor exercise. Jogging in an open park exposes me to natural sunlight and helps clear my head after a stressful day. That being said, during rainy weather, modern indoor gym facilities are extremely convenient."*

#### 🔵 Level 3: Khó (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"I harbor a strong inclination for open-air calisthenics in natural surroundings. Immersing oneself in nature while physically exerting—often termed **green exercise**—imparts a profound synergistic psychological boost that the sterile, fluorescent-lit environment of a commercial gymnasium simply fails to replicate."*
* **Phân tích nâng cấp (Upgrade Analysis):**
  - *exercise outside* ➡️ **open-air calisthenics in natural surroundings / green exercise**.
  - *boring gym* ➡️ **sterile, fluorescent-lit environment of a commercial gymnasium**.

---

### Câu 3: Is it easy to find healthy food where you live?

#### 🟢 Level 1: Dễ (Band 5.0 - 5.5):
> *"Yes, it is very easy. There is a local wet market right behind my house where I can buy fresh vegetables, fish, and fruits every morning at very cheap prices."*

#### 🟡 Level 2: Vừa (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"Generally speaking, yes. My neighborhood has plenty of supermarkets offering organic produce, alongside numerous vegetarian restaurants. However, fast-food stalls and sugary milk tea shops are also everywhere, so one must exercise self-control."*

#### 🔵 Level 3: Khó (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"It presents a dual reality. On one hand, traditional wet markets furnish an abundance of farm-fresh, affordable agrarian produce. On the other hand, the urban landscape is saturated with **obesogenic food environments**—specifically high-fructose beverage chains and ultra-processed street snacks—requiring disciplined cognitive vigilance to resist dietary temptation."*
* **Phân tích nâng cấp (Upgrade Analysis):**
  - *easy but lots of junk food* ➡️ **presents a dual reality / saturated with obesogenic food environments**.
  - *self-control* ➡️ **requires disciplined cognitive vigilance to resist dietary temptation**.

## 🎤 VII. Part 2: Cue Card, Dàn Ý 4 Bước & Full Model Speeches (Cấp Độ Cao & Trừu Tượng)

### Đề bài: Describe a medical or scientific breakthrough that you believe has profound implications for human health
You should say:
- What the breakthrough is and how you learned about it
- How it works in simple terms
- What medical conditions or problems it aims to eradicate
- And explain what ethical or societal questions it raises

#### 🧭 Dàn ý 4 bước (Mindmap 1 phút):
1. **The Scientific Breakthrough:** Kỹ thuật chỉnh sửa gen CRISPR-Cas9 (`CRISPR-Cas9 precision gene editing`).
2. **Mechanism in a Nutshell:** "Kéo phân tử sinh học" cho phép các nhà khoa học cắt và sửa chính xác các đoạn mã DNA bị lỗi (`molecular scissors capable of excising deleterious genetic mutations`).
3. **Medical Target:** Xóa bỏ vĩnh viễn các căn bệnh di truyền quái ác như thiếu máu hồng cầu hình liềm, u xơ nang và một số dạng ung thư (`eradicate monogenic hereditary afflictions such as sickle-cell anemia and cystic fibrosis`).
4. **Bioethical & Societal Dilemma:** Nguy cơ thương mại hóa "trẻ em thiết kế", phân hóa xã hội thành giai cấp gen thượng đẳng (`designer babies, eugenic stratification, weaponized genetic inequality`).

---

#### 🟡 Bài Mẫu Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"I'm going to talk about a revolutionary medical breakthrough: CRISPR gene editing technology.
> 
> I first learned about this innovation while watching a science documentary on Netflix a few years ago. In simple terms, CRISPR acts like a pair of microscopic scissors that allows geneticists to locate, cut out, and replace faulty DNA sequences inside living cells.
> 
> The potential of this technology is immense. It aims to cure hereditary diseases that have plagued humanity for centuries, such as sickle-cell anemia and inherited blindness. Instead of taking expensive pills for an entire lifetime, patients could potentially be cured permanently with a single genetic therapy.
> 
> However, it raises serious ethical concerns. If wealthy parents use gene editing to enhance the physical traits or intelligence of their unborn babies, we could create an unfair society where rich people are genetically superior. Governments must establish strict laws to ensure this technology is only used for treating severe diseases."*

---

#### 🔵 Bài Mẫu Level 3 (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"I would love to articulate the transformative, albeit morally precarious, potential of a monumental biomedical revolution: **CRISPR-Cas9 genomic editing**.
> 
> My fascination with this technology was ignited by an exhaustive investigative report published in *The Lancet*. Fundamentally, CRISPR operates as an astonishingly precise, programmable molecular apparatus that empowers scientists to navigate the labyrinth of human DNA, excise deleterious mutations with pinpoint accuracy, and splice in healthy genetic sequences.
> 
> The therapeutic horizon of this technology is staggering. It holds the key to the outright eradication of devastating **monogenic hereditary disorders**—ranging from Huntington's disease to sickle-cell anemia—transmuting once-terminal genetic death sentences into curable anomalies. Furthermore, oncology researchers are leveraging it to engineer chimeric immune cells that hunt down malignant tumors with unprecedented efficacy.
> 
> Nevertheless, this scientific triumph precipitates a profoundly chilling **bioethical dilemma**. The transition from somatic gene therapy—which cures living patients—to germline modification, which alters the heritable genetic code of future generations, opens Pandora's box to the dystopia of **designer infants and eugenic commodification**. Were access to cognitive and physiological enhancement to become privatized by socioeconomic elites, humanity could face permanent biological caste stratification. Consequently, universal governance and ethical oversight are imperative to prevent our scientific prowess from outpacing our moral wisdom."*

## 🎙️ VIII. Part 3: Câu Hỏi Thảo Luận Mở Rộng & Biện Luận Triết Học (Part 3 High-Difficulty Discussion)

### Q1: Should sovereign states prioritize public funding for high-tech curative medicine or grassroots preventive lifestyle interventions?

#### 🟡 Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"I think governments should focus far more on preventive healthcare. Subsidizing gym memberships, building bicycle lanes, and taxing sugary soft drinks can prevent millions of chronic diseases like diabetes and heart failure, saving billions of dollars that would otherwise be spent on expensive hospital surgeries."*

#### 🔵 Level 3 (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"Fiscal prioritization must tilt decisively toward **prophylactic public health interventions**. While state-of-the-art curative infrastructure—such as oncology radiation suites or ICU facilities—is undeniably indispensable for treating acute emergencies, channeling the preponderance of national health budgets into downstream symptomatic treatment is fiscally unsustainable. 
> 
> The vast majority of modern mortality stems from **chronic lifestyle-mediated pathologies**—namely metabolic dysfunction and cardiovascular decay. Investing in preventive education, enforcing stringent sugar levies, and subsidizing urban active-mobility infrastructure yield vastly superior long-term socioeconomic dividends, mitigating human agony while shielding national treasuries from fiscal exhaustion."*

---

### Q2: What are the bioethical perils if life-extension technologies successfully double the human lifespan for those who can afford it?

#### 🟡 Level 2 (Band 6.0 - 6.5):
> *"If anti-aging treatments are only available to billionaires, it will create unimaginable social injustice. Rich people could live to 150 years old while ordinary workers die young. Furthermore, if nobody dies, the earth will become severely overpopulated, resulting in extreme shortages of food, housing, and clean water."*

#### 🔵 Level 3 (Band 7.5 - 8.5):
> *"Radical longevity breakthroughs would fundamentally shatter the foundational contracts of human civilization. If the ability to halt cellular senescence is monetized within unfettered free markets, the historical democratic equalizer—mortality—will be abolished. 
> 
> We would witness the emergence of an oligarchic gerontocracy: an immortal socioeconomic elite monopolizing capital, political hegemony, and corporate ownership across centuries, effectively suffocating generational turnover. Furthermore, the ecological strain of a non-senescent population on planetary resources would trigger catastrophic geopolitical conflicts over arable land, freshwater, and energetic reserves."*
