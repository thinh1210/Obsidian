# ✍️ 6.2. IELTS Writing Task 2: Introductions & Thesis Statements
*(Khung Mẫu Câu Mở Bài & Tuyên Bố Lập Trường Cho 5 Dạng Bài Task 2)*

> [!NOTE]
> Đoạn Mở bài (*Introduction*) trong IELTS Writing Task 2 chỉ cần **2 câu hoàn chỉnh (tối đa 3 câu)** nhưng mang tính quyết định đến toàn bộ bài viết:
> 1. **Câu 1 (Background Hook & Paraphrase):** Tái khẳng định bối cảnh đề bài bằng ngôn ngữ học thuật, khách quan.
> 2. **Câu 2 (Thesis Statement - Câu Lập trường):** Trả lời trực diện câu hỏi của đề bài, nêu rõ quan điểm xuyên suốt (*clear position throughout the response* — bắt buộc cho tiêu chí Task Response Band 7.0+).

---

## 🏗️ CÔNG THỨC MỞ BÀI CHUẨN 2 CÂU (THE 2-SENTENCE INTRO FORMULA)

```
[CÂU 1: PARAPHRASE ĐỀ BÀI HỌC THUẬT]  ──▶  [CÂU 2: THESIS STATEMENT ĐANH THÉP]
"It is frequently asserted that..."           "While X has merit, I firmly contend that Y because..."
```

---

### 1. Mẫu câu Paraphrase Đề bài (Background Hooks)
Thay vì chép lại nguyên văn đề thi, hãy dùng các khung câu mở đầu khách quan:

* **`It is frequently asserted / contended that [Paraphrased Issue]...`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**It is frequently asserted that** tertiary educational institutions should prioritize vocational job training over theoretical scholarship."*
* **`In recent decades, [Topic] has emerged as a subject of contentious public debate.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"In recent decades, **the pervasive proliferation of algorithmic surveillance in metropolitan centers has emerged as a subject of contentious public debate**."*
* **`A growing body of opinion suggests that [Paraphrased Claim]...`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**A growing body of opinion suggests that** international tourism inflicts irreversible damage on fragile cultural heritages."*
* **`While [Trend] was historically considered [Adjective], modern commentators increasingly argue that [Counter-trend]...`**

---

### 2. Thesis Statements Cho 5 Dạng Đề Task 2

---

#### 📌 Dạng 1: Agree or Disagree (Quan điểm Đồng tình hay Phản đối)
Cần khẳng định rõ bạn đồng ý, phản đối hay có quan điểm cân bằng (nhưng nghiêng về một bên):

* **Mẫu Hoàn toàn Phản đối (Total Disagreement):**
  * **`While acknowledging the rationale behind this proposition, I firmly dispute the notion that [X], contending instead that [Y].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**While acknowledging the rationale behind this proposition, I firmly dispute the notion that** space exploration represents a wasteful expenditure, **contending instead that** extraterrestrial research yields indispensable technological innovations for sustainable life on Earth."*
* **Mẫu Hoàn toàn Đồng tình (Total Agreement):**
  * **`I wholeheartedly concur with this viewpoint, as [Primary Reason A], and moreover, [Secondary Reason B].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**I wholeheartedly concur with this viewpoint, as** early childhood bilingualism accelerates cognitive neuroplasticity, **and moreover**, equips individuals with vital intercultural competencies for a globalized economy."*
* **Mẫu Cân bằng có xu hướng (Nuanced / Balanced Agreement):**
  * **`While it is undeniable that [Merit of Side A], I am convinced that [Dominant Merit of Side B] carries significantly greater societal weight.`**

---

#### 📌 Dạng 2: Discuss Both Views and Give Your Opinion (Thảo luận 2 Quan điểm)
Giám khảo bắt buộc bạn phải thể hiện quan điểm của bản thân **ngay từ Mở bài**:

* **Mẫu Nghiêng về Quan điểm 2 (Leaning towards View B):**
  * **`While persuasive arguments can be marshaled in support of the premise that [View A], I side with those who maintain that [View B] represents a vastly superior paradigm.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**While persuasive arguments can be marshaled in support of the premise that** economic growth should take precedence over conservation, **I side with those who maintain that** environmental sustainability represents an indispensable prerequisite for long-term human prosperity."*
* **Mẫu Nghiêng về Quan điểm 1 (Leaning towards View A):**
  * **`Although proponents of [View B] raise valid considerations regarding [Concern], I firmly endorse the contention that [View A] offers the most viable long-term trajectory.`**

---

#### 📌 Dạng 3: Causes & Solutions / Problems & Solutions (Nguyên nhân & Giải pháp)

* **`This essay will examine the principal catalysts underlying this alarming trajectory — foremost among which are [Cause A] and [Cause B] — before delineating viable remedial policies to mitigate its adverse repercussions.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**This essay will examine the principal catalysts underlying this alarming trajectory — foremost among which are** urban economic centralisation and defective public transit infrastructure — **before delineating viable remedial policies to mitigate its adverse repercussions**."*
* **`This analysis elucidates the primary socioeconomic drivers of [Problem] and puts forward a multifaceted framework of state-led interventions to alleviate the crisis.`**

---

#### 📌 Dạng 4: Advantages and Disadvantages (Ưu điểm vs. Nhược điểm)

* **Mẫu Ưu điểm Áp đảo Nhược điểm (Advantages Outweigh Disadvantages):**
  * **`While acknowledging the non-trivial drawbacks associated with [Topic], such as [Drawback], I am convinced that the prospective dividends — particularly [Advantage A] and [Advantage B] — decisively outweigh these vulnerabilities.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**While acknowledging the non-trivial drawbacks associated with** remote employment paradigms, such as professional isolation, **I am convinced that the prospective dividends — particularly** operational flexibility and eliminated commuting overheads — **decisively outweigh these vulnerabilities**."*
* **Mẫu Nhược điểm Nghiêm trọng hơn:**
  * **`Notwithstanding the ostensible conveniences conferred by [Topic], I argue that its acute socio-ecological tolls render it an ultimately deleterious development.`**

---

#### 📌 Dạng 5: Two-part Questions (Câu hỏi Kép)

* **`This essay addresses both dimensions of this inquiry, arguing that while [Answer to Question 1], the most efficacious response involves [Answer to Question 2].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**This essay addresses both dimensions of this inquiry, arguing that while** pervasive academic stress constitutes the primary driver of student depression, **the most efficacious institutional response involves** restructuring high-stakes examination architectures in favor of continuous assessment."*

## 🎯 BỘ VÍ DỤ ĐỐI CHIẾU MỞ BÀI HOÀN CHỈNH

### Đề bài: *Some people believe that unpaid community service should be a compulsory part of high school programmes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?*

> ❌ **Mở bài Band 5.5 (Dịch thô, đại từ thừa, thiếu từ vựng học thuật):**
> *"Nowadays, many people think that students should do unpaid community service in high school. In my opinion, I agree with this idea because it helps them learn good things and get ready for the future."*

> ✍️ **Mở bài Band 8.5 (Cấu trúc học thuật, Thesis đanh thép):**
> *"**It is frequently asserted that** mandatory volunteer civic engagement should be formally integrated into secondary school curricula. **I wholeheartedly concur with this educational philosophy, as** altruistic service cultivates civic empathy among adolescent cohorts, **and moreover**, provides indispensable experiential competencies that cannot be replicated through traditional classroom pedagogies."*
