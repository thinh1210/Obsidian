 ✍️ 6.1. IELTS Writing Task 1: Data Trends, Comparisons & Processes
*(Khung Mẫu Câu Học Thuật Báo Cáo Biểu Đồ, So Sánh Số Liệu, Bản Đồ & Quy Trình)*

> [!NOTE]
> Trong **IELTS Writing Task 1**, nhiệm vụ của bạn là viết một báo cáo học thuật khách quan dài tối thiểu 150 từ. Bạn **tuyệt đối không được đưa ý kiến cá nhân** (*không dùng "I think", "luckily"*), mà phải sử dụng các cấu trúc:
> * **Paraphrase đề bài chuẩn xác**
> * **Câu Overview (Tổng quan) sắc bén** nắm bắt xu hướng chính và điểm nổi bật nhất
> * **Mẫu câu miêu tả xu hướng biến động (Trends)**, **so sánh đối chiếu (Comparisons)**, và **quy trình/bản đồ (Processes & Maps)**.

---

## 📑 BỘ KHUNG MẪU CÂU THEO TỪNG PHẦN TASK 1

```
[BƯỚC 1: PARAPHRASE ĐỀ BÀI] ──▶ [BƯỚC 2: CÂU OVERVIEW TỔNG QUAN] ──▶ [BƯỚC 3: CHI TIẾT SỐ LIỆU & SO SÁNH]
"The line chart delineates..."  "Overall, what stands out from the data is..."  "X experienced an exponential surge, whereas Y..."
```

---

### 1. Mẫu câu Paraphrase Đề bài (Introduction Sentences)
Tuyệt đối không chép lại đề bài; thay thế các động từ và cụm từ theo công thức:
`[Loại biểu đồ] + [Động từ học thuật] + [Đối tượng nghiên cứu] + [Địa điểm] + [Khoảng thời gian]`

* **Động từ học thuật thay thế cho "shows / gives information about":**
  * **`delineates`** /dɪˈlɪn.i.eɪts/ *(v)* — Phác họa rõ nét quỹ đạo biến động
  * **`illustrates / depicts`** *(v)* — Minh họa trực quan
  * **`compares [X] in terms of [Y]`** — So sánh các đối tượng dựa trên tiêu chí Y
  * **`provides a breakdown of [Noun]`** — Phân tách các cấu phần chi tiết

#### Các mẫu câu hoàn chỉnh:
* **`The line graph delineates the trajectory of [Metric] in [Countries] over a [Number]-year period from [Year] to [Year].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**The line graph delineates the trajectory of** renewable energy adoption in three European nations over a fifteen-year period from 2010 to 2025."*
* **`The bar chart provides a comparative breakdown of [Metric] categorized by [Age group / Gender / Sector] in [Year].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**The bar chart provides a comparative breakdown of** household expenditure categorized by income deciles in the United Kingdom in 2023."*
* **`The pie charts illustrate the proportional allocation of [Metric] across various sectors in [Year].`**

---

### 2. Mẫu câu Viết Overview Đỉnh Cao (The Master Overview Frame)
Câu Overview là linh hồn của Task 1 — thiếu Overview bạn không bao giờ vượt qua Band 5.0 tiêu chí Task Achievement!

* **`Overall, what stands out from the data is that [Trend 1], whereas [Trend 2]. Additionally, [Highest / Lowest figure] consistently accounted for the dominant share.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**Overall, what stands out from the data is that** solar and wind generation experienced exponential upward trajectories, **whereas** coal consumption underwent a steady contraction. **Additionally**, fossil fuels consistently accounted for the dominant share of total energy output throughout the timeframe."*
* **`In general, a general upward / downward trend was discernible across virtually all categories, with the notable exception of [Outlier].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**In general, a general upward trend was discernible across virtually all economic sectors, with the notable exception of** heavy manufacturing."*
* **`Looking from an overall perspective, the period witnessed a radical restructuring of [Metric], culminating in [Key result].`**

### 3. Mẫu câu Miêu tả Xu hướng Biến động Dữ liệu (Dynamic Trends)

#### A. Xu hướng Tăng trưởng (Upward Trends):
* **`[Subject] witnessed / experienced an exponential surge, climbing steeply from [X] to [Y].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"Global electric vehicle sales **witnessed an exponential surge**, climbing steeply from 2.1 million units in 2020 to over 14 million by 2024."*
* **`There was a steady upward trajectory in [Metric], which culminated in a peak of [Number] in [Year].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**There was a steady upward trajectory in** university enrollments, **which culminated in a peak of** 450,000 students in 2022."*
* **`[Metric] escalated markedly, nearly tripling / quadrupling over the recorded duration.`**

#### B. Xu hướng Suy giảm (Downward Trends):
* **`[Subject] underwent a precipitous decline, plummeting to a nadir of [Number] in [Year].`**
  * *Ngữ cảnh:* Giảm dốc đứng chạm đáy (*nadir = điểm thấp nhất*).
  * *Ví dụ:* *"Domestic airline patronage **underwent a precipitous decline**, plummeting to a nadir of 12,000 passengers during the peak quarantine quarter."*
* **`A downward trend was registered in [Metric], dropping progressively from [X] to [Y].`**
* **`[Metric] slumped substantially, before bottoming out at [Number] midway through the period.`**

#### C. Biến động & Đi ngang (Fluctuations & Plateaus):
* **`[Metric] oscillated erratically within a narrow band of [X%] to [Y%] throughout the decade.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"Crude oil prices **oscillated erratically within a narrow band of** \$65 to \$78 per barrel throughout the decade."*
* **`Following a period of volatility, [Metric] plateaued at approximately [Number] for the remainder of the timeline.`**

---

### 4. Mẫu câu So sánh, Tỷ lệ & Xếp hạng (Static Comparisons & Proportions)

* **`[Category A] accounted for / constituted the lion's share of [Total], standing at [X%], whereas [Category B] was dwarfed by comparison at a mere [Y%].`**
  * *Ngữ cảnh:* Một bên chiếm phần lớn (*lion's share*), một bên lép vế hoàn toàn (*dwarfed*).
  * *Ví dụ:* *"Smartphones **accounted for the lion's share of** consumer gadget revenues, standing at 68%, **whereas** digital cameras **were dwarfed by comparison at a mere** 4%."*
* **`[Metric A] was nearly double / threefold / five times that of [Metric B], recording figures of [X] and [Y] respectively.`**
* **`[Category A] outstripped / surpassed all competitors, securing the preeminent rank throughout the examined duration.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"Export revenues from electronics **consistently outstripped** textile receipts, securing the preeminent rank throughout the decade."*

---

### 5. Mẫu câu Bản đồ & Quy trình (Maps & Process Diagrams)

#### A. Bản đồ (Maps & Urban Transformations):
* **`Over the [Number]-year period, the rural hamlet was transformed into a bustling commercial hub, with [Feature] being replaced by [New infrastructure].`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"Over the 20-year period, the dockland area **was transformed into a bustling commercial hub, with** derelict warehouses **being replaced by** high-density residential towers."*
* **`The southern quadrant witnessed extensive modernization, marked by the construction of [Facility] adjacent to [Location].`**
* **`Industrial facilities were entirely demolished to make way for pedestrianized public recreational zones.`**

#### B. Quy trình sản xuất / Vòng đời (Processes):
* **`The cyclical process commences with the extraction of raw materials, followed by a sequence of chemical refining stages, culminating in the automated packaging of the finished merchandise.`**
  * *Ví dụ:* *"**The recycling process commences with** mechanical sorting, **followed by a sequence of** thermal decontamination stages, **culminating in** the extrusion of reusable plastic pellets."*
* **`Once [Step A] is completed, the intermediate mixture is channeled into [Component B], wherein it undergoes [Action].`**

## 🎯 ĐOẠN VĂN TASK 1 MẪU HOÀN CHỈNH

> ✍️ **Band 8.5 Task 1 Body Paragraph (Comparative Trends):**
> *"**The line graph delineates the trajectory of** carbon dioxide emissions produced by three transport sectors in the United Kingdom between 1990 and 2020. 
> 
> **Overall, what stands out from the data is that** while maritime and rail emissions **experienced steady downward trajectories**, road transport emissions **witnessed a relentless upward surge**. **Additionally**, road vehicles **consistently accounted for the lion's share of** atmospheric emissions throughout the entire timeline.
> 
> In 1990, road transport emissions stood at 110 million tonnes. Over the subsequent two decades, this figure **escalated markedly, climbing to a peak of** 135 million tonnes in 2018, before dipping marginally to 128 million tonnes by 2020. **Conversely, rail emissions exhibited a diametrically opposed trajectory**; having started at 15 million tonnes, they **underwent a precipitous decline**, plummeting to a negligible 3 million tonnes by the end of the period, **dwarfed by** vehicular road emissions by a factor of more than forty."*
